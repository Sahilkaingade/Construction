Horizon Luxury Villas

Miami, USA · 2024

Project Overview

A collection of custom-designed luxury villas in Miami, crafted for comfort, privacy and effortless indoor-outdoor living.

Horizon Luxury Villas brings together architectural design and high-end craftsmanship in a gated residential community. Each villa was built to a refined standard, with careful attention to materials, finishes and the way light moves through the home.

Open-plan living spaces, private pools and landscaped gardens make the most of the Florida climate, while hurricane-resistant construction keeps every home safe and durable for years to come.

Project Highlights

  • Custom architectural design for every villa
  • Open-plan living with indoor-outdoor flow
  • Private pools and landscaped gardens
  • Storm-resistant, long-life construction
