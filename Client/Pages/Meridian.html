Commercial

Meridian Business Tower

New York, USA · 2024

Project Overview

A modern corporate tower rising in the heart of New York's business district, designed for companies that want a premium address and a workplace built for the future.

Meridian Business Tower was delivered as a full commercial build, from foundation and structure through to the finished glass facade and interiors. Our team coordinated closely with the client, architects and city authorities to keep the programme on schedule in one of the most demanding construction environments in the country.

Inside, the tower offers flexible, light-filled floor plates, high-speed elevator systems and efficient building services, giving tenants room to grow and adapt their space over time.

Project Highlights

  • Premium glass and steel facade
  • Flexible, column-light office floors
  • Energy-efficient building systems
  • Delivered on schedule in a dense urban site
