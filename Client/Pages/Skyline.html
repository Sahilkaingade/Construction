Skyline Office Park

Los Angeles, USA · 2023

Project Overview

A modern office park in Los Angeles, supported by new roads, utilities and site infrastructure built to serve it for decades.

Skyline Office Park combined civil works with building construction. Before the offices could rise, our teams delivered the access roads, drainage, utilities and parking that the whole campus depends on.

The result is a connected, well-landscaped business campus with efficient circulation, reliable services and room for future expansion.

Project Highlights

  • New access roads and site utilities
  • Integrated drainage and parking
  • Landscaped, walkable campus layout
  • Designed with room for future expansion
