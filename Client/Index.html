Our Story

Foundations Built On Integrity & Craft

Founded in 1996 by Marcus Harrington in New York, Buildstone began as a small residential contractor. Through relentless commitment to quality and transparency, we've grown into one of America's most respected construction groups.

Today, 1,200+ skilled professionals operate across 18 states, delivering everything from iconic towers to master-planned communities and critical infrastructure.
